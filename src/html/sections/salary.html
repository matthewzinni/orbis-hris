Private — visible only to Matthew Zinni. Salary history comes from Employee Navigator.

How to refresh from Employee Navigator
  1. Employee Navigator has no company-wide salary history report; each employee's Employee Record → Compensation page has its own "Export CSV".
  2. Combine them into one CSV with these columns: First Name, Last Name, Hire Date, Employment Status, Salary Type, Effective Date, Annual Base Salary, Hourly Rate, Hours Per Week, Annual Benefit Salary, Change Reason.
  3. Import it here, review the matches, then Save import. Delete the CSV afterwards.
