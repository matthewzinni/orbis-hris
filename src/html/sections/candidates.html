Candidate pipeline
How we hire Cole approves → HR screen → supervisor interview → decision in 5 business days
  1. A need comes in. An employee brings Matthew a referral, or a team lead asks for another employee. Employee or lead → Matthew
  2. Cole approves the hire. Matthew confirms with Cole that we can afford it. No one contacts the candidate yet. If Cole says no, the process stops and Matthew lets the employee or lead who asked know. Matthew + Cole
  3. Screening interview. Once Cole approves, Matthew reaches out to the candidate to set up and hold the first interview. Matthew
  4. Supervisor interview. One more interview with the supervisor of the department. This is the last interview. Department supervisor
  5. Decision within 5 business days of the supervisor interview: move the candidate to Offer or Rejected. Supervisor + Matthew
  6. Keep the C-Suite leader informed. The C-Suite leader over the department knows about the candidate and hears the final decision. Matthew
  • No interviews until Cole approves.
  • Two interviews max.
  • Decide within 5 business days.
  • C-Suite leader stays informed.

Track each step in the candidate's Hiring Checklist on their Profile tab. Orbis alerts you when a decision is due.
